# Known limitations

CatchWave is experimental. A single OnePlus 12 manual pilot accepted LAN audio/gestures, including held volume in Android versionCode 25. design-qa.md records physical acceptance of Host 0.1.32 and USB-tethered audio as pending. Broader reliability, latency, quality and screen-off battery behavior remain unmeasured.

- Host capture uses the current default multimedia output. No independent CatchWave endpoint, selector, discovery, route switch, resampling, internet relay or firewall installer exists.
- AudioRelay parity is not established. Existing virtual output can be captured, but no AudioRelay source/private protocol is included. Tilt controls local Android media volume.
- WASAPI supports mono/stereo PCM at 44100/48000/96000 Hz. Unsupported formats fail explicitly. Stalls drop stale audio; seamless recovery and end-to-end latency are not guaranteed.
- While control is active, ordinary phone handling can trigger commands. An owner pickup caused tilt to reduce volume to silence. Keep control off outside deliberate use/testing. Builds after versionCode 29 added a posture-change neutral guard and replaced timed gesture arming with explicit button activation; versionCode 53 narrowed the posture guard so that pickup and display rotation keep tap/shake commands ready. None of these has demonstrated a reliable reduction in accidental input, and false-positive rates are unmeasured. Any sharp jolt with little rotation, including setting the phone down or knocking the surface under it, may count as a tap. It is not production-ready.
- Gestures need neutral/fresh sensors. Held native volume steps repeat at most every 400 ms. Motion/posture/OEM behavior can affect detection. Windows player support determines media-key effects; ACK is not proof of playback transition.
- Activation is button-only: no pose and no expiry, and phone position never turns control on or off. «Активировать управление» needs control allowed on both the Host and the phone, «Разрешить жесты» on, a session receiving fresh audio, a CatchWave screen in the foreground (or the screen-off option) and fresh accel/gyro/orientation samples. After activation, hold still for 1.5 s once to set neutral; rotating the phone during the hold restarts it, and no command responds before it completes. Taps and shake then stay ready through pickup and display rotation. A swing of more than about 35 degrees off the volume-tilt axis (for example pickup or laying the phone down) pauses only tilt volume until the phone stays still for 0.3 s, then rebases its axis. A display rotation rebases at once but discards unrecognized taps/shake, a held tilt and commands not yet sent to the PC, and ignores input for about 0.25 s. Tilt volume does not step while the screen is within about 15 degrees of flat (tilting further from flat still works); if neutral is set with the phone held sideways and the screen not rotated, tilt stays unavailable until the display rotates or neutral is reset. Control turns off on sensor gaps over 150 ms, stale/invalid/unreliable sensors, any change of either control permission, turning gestures off, an audio stall of 0.75 s or more (even if audio resumes), leaving or pausing the CatchWave screen without the screen-off option, session loss, «Выключить управление» and «Сбросить активацию и нейтраль»; recovery never reactivates it. Sensors measure orientation and motion only, not table contact, pickup or human intent. Thresholds need physical testing.
- Diagnostics retain at most 256 metadata events locally. Disk failures keep RAM history and increment a counter; the last up-to-1 s of events can be lost on abrupt process kill. There is no audio recording or automatic log upload.
- Optional screen-off gestures use a foreground service and partial wake lock. Long runs, battery cost and Bluetooth routes need testing. Android power management can stop the process.
- Host restart invalidates pairing. Scan a new private QR locally. Never publish pairing data/signing keys.
- The experimental APK uses a local debug signer. Different signers prevent in-place updates. Production signing and a new binary GitHub Release are outside this source update.
- Recognition uses third-party HTTPS and multiple consistent fragments. Matching metadata can select another master/remix/live version. YouTube availability varies by account/region.
- Timeline alignment/acoustic refinement remain experimental; millisecond synchronization is not guaranteed. Handoff seeks to estimated position without adding callback lag. Silent/denied capture invalidates acoustic refinement. Live Sync requires headphones/media-session access; queue/metadata gaps can need user intervention.
- iOS, continuous acoustic Live Sync refinement and automatic version disambiguation are not implemented.
- Android minSdk30 is a declared installation/API baseline, not evidence for every model. Accel+gyro are required for all gestures; without either gestures are disabled. Missing game rotation vector falls back to rotation vector; absence of both blocks activation and all gesture commands, because activation needs fresh orientation samples. The current QR pairing needs a camera. Windows ARM64/x86, Linux/macOS Host and other Android models are not physically validated; no Linux/macOS Host or iOS client exists.
